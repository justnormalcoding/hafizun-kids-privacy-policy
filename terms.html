02A practice aid, with human guidance

The app helps with listening, repetition, recall and review. It does not guarantee memorization, learning speed, retention or any religious outcome. Progress indicators, gardens and adult confirmations record practice activity; they are not qualifications or judgments of a child’s faith or achievement.

Speech recognition can mishear, miss errors or flag correct words. It does not assess tajweed or certify a recitation. A parent or qualified Quran teacher should listen and provide correction. Do not use automated feedback to shame, punish or pressure a child. Keep sessions and listening volume appropriate for the child, with breaks as needed.

If you notice a Quran-display or audio-reference problem, stop using the affected passage, check an authoritative mushaf or qualified teacher, and report the issue without sending child data.
