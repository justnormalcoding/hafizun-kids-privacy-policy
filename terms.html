With a grown-up

A parent, guardian or authorised adult must set up, manage and actively supervise each child’s session, including permissions, backups and sharing. The app does not verify adult presence.

Free to use

Hafizun Kids is free, with no subscription or in-app purchases. Use it for personal, family or supervised learning. Please respect others’ rights and don’t misuse the app or recitation services.

A helping hand for learning

The app supports practice; it does not guarantee memorization or assess tajweed. Voice checks can make mistakes. A parent or Quran teacher should listen and guide the child. Features may change or occasionally be unavailable.
