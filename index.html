01Made for children. Managed by adults.

Hafizun Kids is a free Quran memorization app made for children, including children under 13. This policy describes the current iOS app and this policy website. “We” and “us” refer to TawheedTech, the app provider.

A parent or legal guardian must set up and manage the app and actively supervise every learning session. Another responsible adult may help with the guardian’s permission. Adults handle permissions, downloads, sharing, backups and privacy choices. We do not remotely watch or supervise children.

The app does not verify an adult’s identity or continuously check their presence. Its simple maths question is a prompt for adult involvement, not identity verification or legally verified parental consent. Adult supervision does not remove children’s privacy rights or our responsibilities under applicable law.

02Profiles and learning progress

The app stores family profiles, a chosen name or nickname, avatar, learning preferences, reciter, language, practice history, review dates, saved lesson and garden progress on your device. Reminder settings include the chosen time, days and time zone. Local identifiers keep each child’s progress separate.

Use a nickname rather than a child’s full name. We do not ask for a date of birth, address, school, child’s email, phone number or precise location. We do not have an app account system or a server that receives these profiles or learning records.

Device backups may include profiles and progress, depending on your Apple or computer-backup settings. A local recovery copy of family data is kept to help recover from an interrupted or damaged save.

03Optional recording and voice checks

Recording is optional and off by default. An adult must enable it and grant microphone permission before a child records. Recordings are used for local playback. If an adult also enables voice checks, Apple’s on-device speech technology checks the recorded Arabic words against the practice text.

The app does not upload recordings or transcripts. Transcripts are used temporarily for feedback and are not saved by the app. There is no cloud-recognition fallback. Compatible hardware, iOS and Arabic language support are needed; Apple may need an internet connection to download a speech model during setup.

New lesson recordings are temporary. The app deletes them on lesson exit or backgrounding and clears abandoned temporary recordings at launch. The current lesson does not offer permanent saving. Recordings saved by an older version remain until deleted. Recording folders are excluded from device backups and recordings are not included in exported family backups.

You can switch off recording or voice checks in the app and revoke Microphone or Speech Recognition permission in iOS Settings. Children can continue reading and practising aloud without either permission. Voice feedback can be wrong and is not a tajweed assessment.

04Internet and recitation downloads

The Quran text is bundled with the app. Recitation downloads use AlQuran Cloud / Islamic Network: api.alquran.cloud provides the selected reciter and ayah reference, and cdn.islamic.network delivers audio. Downloads are cached for later use. Already downloaded lesson audio can be used offline.

These providers and their delivery infrastructure receive the requested reciter/ayah, your IP address and ordinary request details such as request time. The app does not include child names, profiles, progress, recordings or transcripts in those requests. We do not receive the providers’ server logs through the app.

Providers control their own technical logs and retention. We cannot promise that they keep no logs. Their services may operate outside your country. An adult should prepare downloads and then use downloaded audio offline when appropriate. See AlQuran Cloud’s service terms.

05Gentle, optional reminders

With adult approval and iOS notification permission, the app schedules local Quran-practice reminders using the selected time and days. No app push-notification server or advertising network is used. Reminders can be disabled in the app or iOS Settings. Notifications may appear on the lock screen according to your device settings.

06Backups and adult-controlled sharing

An adult can export a family backup containing profiles, nicknames, settings and progress. It is encrypted with a separate password chosen for that file. There is no app sign-in password. Keep the backup password safely: we cannot recover it. Recordings and downloaded recitations are excluded. Restoring replaces current family profiles and progress and turns reminders off until configured again.

Exporting uses the iOS share sheet, so the adult chooses the destination and any recipient. A shared garden milestone includes the child’s nickname and milestone information. Review it before sharing. Files and messages sent to another app, cloud drive or person are then also subject to that destination’s practices. We cannot recall or delete those copies for you.

07Keeping and deleting data

  • Profiles and progress: remain locally until reset, a profile is removed, data is replaced by a restore, or the app is deleted. Resetting progress keeps the profile’s settings.
  • Local recovery copy: a previous saved state is retained and replaced as new saves occur. Removing a profile from active data may not immediately remove it from that recovery copy.
  • Recordings: new takes are temporary as explained above. Older saved recordings can be deleted from Saved recordings when that page is available; deleting a child’s profile also deletes that child’s saved recordings.
  • Downloaded recitations: remain in the app’s cache until removed, reclaimed by the system or the app is deleted.
  • Exported and device backups: remain wherever the adult or operating system saved them until separately removed.

To remove the app’s entire local container, use Delete App in iOS, not Offload App. Manage iCloud/computer backups and exported files separately; reinstalling from a backup can restore earlier data. We cannot remotely retrieve or delete the data kept only on your device.

08No advertising or behavioural tracking

The current app has no advertisements, advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, social network, public profiles or chat. We do not sell family data, use it for targeted advertising, or upload recordings to train AI models. The app does not request access to contacts, camera, photos or precise location. Selecting a backup file does not give the app unrestricted access to your other files.

09Contacting us

Support is for adults. If you email us, we receive the email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. Use only the information needed to describe the problem. Do not send a child’s voice recording, full name, photo, school, backup file or other sensitive information. Redact screenshots first.

Support information is used to respond, troubleshoot, handle privacy requests and meet applicable legal obligations—not for advertising. We keep it only while needed for those purposes and then delete it, except where a specific legal obligation or unresolved dispute requires retention. If unnecessary child information reaches us, we will take steps to remove it rather than use it for another purpose. Email is handled through our email provider, Google.

10This website and other service providers

This policy site is hosted by GitHub Pages. We have added no advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, embedded media or cookies. The page loads its design files locally from the same site. GitHub may process IP addresses and technical request information to deliver and secure the site under its privacy statement.

Apple separately operates the App Store, iOS permissions, speech-model delivery and any Apple backups or diagnostics you enable. Those services follow Apple’s settings and privacy information. Email, hosting, recitation providers and destinations you choose may process information in other countries. Their own retention and legal arrangements apply; this policy does not promise they receive no technical data.

11Parent choices, rights and security

An adult can review and edit profiles locally, reset progress, delete a profile, remove older recordings, switch off optional features, revoke iOS permissions and stop using the app. Where applicable law gives you access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction, portability or consent-withdrawal rights over information we hold, contact us. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority. We may need reasonable verification of an adult’s authority, without requesting unnecessary child information.

Device security matters: use a device passcode, protect backup passwords and limit who can access the device or exported files. The app uses HTTPS for recitation requests, local iOS storage protections and encryption for exported backups. No app, device or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

We do not treat a child’s tap, the maths gate or an iOS permission prompt as a substitute for verifiable parental consent where the law requires it. Any future collection requiring such consent must have the necessary notice and consent process before it begins.

12Updates and privacy contact

We will date changes on this page. Material changes to children’s data handling require appropriate notice and any consent required by law before the changed processing starts. Posting a new policy alone does not authorise a new use of a child’s information.

07الاحتفاظ بالبيانات وحذفها
